Step 1 · Search

Check a place before you leave home

Search any mall, place of worship or beach. You'll see if it's accessible, partly accessible or not, and the reason why: the ramp, the doors, the toilet.

Try it on the phone, or tap an example:

Sample places and ratings for this demo.

Step 2 · Review

Ratings come from people who were there

After a visit, RollSpot asks a few short questions. Was there a ramp? Could you push up it without help? Is the toilet big enough to turn around in? It takes about two minutes.

Step 3 · Share

One visit helps everyone who comes after

Each answer joins the place's page, free for anyone to read. You see how many people found the ramp easy, what they noticed, and how recent their visit was.

Impact

A map for everyone who checks the steps first

Wheelchair users. Parents with strollers. People on crutches after an injury. Older relatives who can't manage stairs. One honest map helps all of them get out the door.

1.3 billion people
live with a significant disability.

That's about 1 in 6 of us. Every review on RollSpot makes one more place easier to plan for.Source: World Health Organization, Global report on health equity for persons with disabilities, 2022.

Team

Built with the people who face the steps

We are a small team of designers and developers in Bali. Wheelchair users, parents and caregivers help us decide which questions to ask, because they know what makes a visit work and what ruins it.
