Rollspot Account

Delete your Rollspot account

You can delete your account at any time. It happens straight away and can't be undone.

In the app
  1. Open Rollspot and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile, then Settings.
  3. Tap My Account, then Delete Account, and confirm.

Can't open the app?

Email __CONTACT_EMAIL__ from the address on your Apple or Google account, with the subject "Delete my Rollspot account". We'll delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email, name and the link to your Apple or Google sign-in.
  • Your profile: display name, profile photo, nickname and mobility aids.
  • Every review, answer, note and photo you contributed.
  • Your saved places, notification settings and notification tokens.

Nothing is kept afterwards, apart from copies in our hosting providers' backups, which are overwritten within 30 days. Places themselves (names and locations from OpenStreetMap) stay on the map; they were never yours to begin with.

Only want to remove a review?

Open the review from your profile and delete it there; your account stays.
