Rollspot Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Rollspot is a map of how accessible places are, built from reviews by people who visit them. This page explains what the Rollspot apps for iPhone and Android collect, why, and how you can delete it. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and name, and a password (stored only as a secure hash) or your Apple or Google sign-in. Apple and Google tell us your email and name; we never see your Apple or Google password.
  • Your profile: an optional display name and profile photo, a random nickname we create for you, and the mobility aids you choose to list (for example a wheelchair). Mobility aids are optional; we use them only to label your reviews (for example "Wheelchair user") so readers know whose experience they're reading.
  • What you contribute: your reviews, answers, notes and photos of places.
  • Saved places and your notification settings.
  • A notification token for your phone, if you allow notifications, so we can send them.

Your location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to centre the map and find places near you. It is sent with each search to find nearby places, and is not stored with your account or kept as a history. You can use Rollspot without sharing your location by moving the map yourself.

What other people can see

Reviews and photos are public: anyone using Rollspot can see them, with your display name (or your nickname if you haven't set one), your profile photo and your mobility-aid label. Your email address, saved places and settings are never shown to anyone.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show places and their accessibility, and publish your reviews.
  • To send the notifications you've switched on: new reviews at places you saved, a reminder to finish a review you started, and occasional news from Rollspot. Each can be turned off in Settings → Notifications.
  • To keep the service safe, for example by removing spam or abusive content.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run Rollspot for us, and only what each one needs:

  • Cloudflare hosts our servers, database and photo storage.
  • Google Firebase delivers push notifications (your notification token and the notification text).
  • Apple and Google, if you sign in with them.
  • Map providers: the iPhone app shows Apple Maps; the Android app loads map images from OpenFreeMap. Like any website, they see your device's IP address and which part of the map you're looking at.

Place names and locations come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors). We don't send OpenStreetMap anything about you.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and contributions until you delete them. Deleting a review removes it and its photos. Deleting your account removes everything listed under "What we collect" straight away, including all your reviews and photos. Copies in our hosting providers' backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your profile, reviews and settings in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Settings → My Account → Delete Account. Can't open the app? See how to delete your account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or anything else about your privacy, at __CONTACT_EMAIL__. We reply within 30 days.

Children

Rollspot isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has made an account, write to us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is important.
